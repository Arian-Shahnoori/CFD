\begin{abstract}
A numerical solver is developed for the one-dimensional linear advection equation with constant transport velocity. The equation is discretized in space using a finite-volume formulation, with first-order upwind, second-order upwind, and second-order central-difference schemes. Time integration is performed using the explicit midpoint Runge--Kutta method (RK2) and the classical fourth-order Runge--Kutta method (RK4). Numerical solutions are verified against the analytical solution, and grid-refinement studies are used to assess spatial accuracy and the observed convergence order. Fourier-mode analysis is used to investigate the linear stability of selected space--time discretizations and to derive their limiting Courant--Friedrichs--Lewy (CFL) numbers. Further computational studies examine the effects of spatial discretization, CFL number, and temporal integration order, including the role of spatial-error dominance in the observed convergence behavior.
\end{abstract}
